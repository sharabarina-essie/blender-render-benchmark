Scored region

Margins in source pixels. Exclude FPS counters, window borders or other changing UI. Images are never resized for scoring.

Tolerance and evidence

per channel, 0–255

A pixel is changed when any RGB channel differs by more than the tolerance. Tolerance does not change MAE, RMSE, PSNR or SSIM.

How to use and interpret this comparison
  • Load an approved render as A and the image under test as B. Match camera, animation frame, resolution, color management and render settings.
  • Use the wipe slider or blink to find missing objects and shifted edges. Inspect at 100% or above for pixelation, gray strips and texture detail. Side-by-side views share zoom and pan.
  • Difference shows absolute RGB error, multiplied by Gain for visibility. Gain does not affect metrics. The saved difference is full-size, black outside the scored region, with no viewer decorations.
  • Metrics use browser-decoded 8-bit sRGB, composited over black; alpha is also scored separately. Browser color conversion and rounding can affect results. This is a display-image tool, not a linear-light, HDR or raw-file comparator. Export EXR/HDR/16-bit renders to matched sRGB PNGs first; raw high-depth differences need a separate workflow.
  • SSIM uses non-overlapping 8×8 luminance blocks (Rec.709 weights), population variance, C1 = 2.55² and C2 = 7.65². Partial edge blocks are included, weighted by pixel count. This variant may differ from other SSIM tools.
  • SSIM and PSNR describe similarity; they cannot prove correct rendering or identify a compression codec. Denoising, noise seeds and different viewpoints can change the scores. No automatic pass/fail is assigned.
  • PNG, JPEG and WebP are accepted. Animated images, SVG, EXR, HDR and other formats are outside this tool’s scope. Each image is limited to 40 million pixels. Files are read locally; the tool contains no remote scripts or network calls.
  • Keyboard shortcuts in the viewer: F fit, 1 actual pixels, + / − zoom. For native versus remote QA, compare saved renders and displayed screenshots as separate checks.
